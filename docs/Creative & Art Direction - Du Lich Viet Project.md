# **Creative & Art Direction \- Du Lich Viet Project**

**Theme:** "A Window to Vietnam \- Clear & Refined"

## **Creative Vision**

We are not building a tour booking website. We are crafting a **digital window**, where every click and every scroll opens up an inspiring view of Vietnam's beauty. The user experience must feel **elegant, serene, and premium**, like flipping through the pages of a luxury travel magazine, not getting lost in a noisy marketplace of information.

The goal is to evoke a **desire to explore** long before the user even thinks about booking a ticket.

## **Guiding Art Principles**

### **1\. The "Sheet of Glass" Principle**

**Mindset:** The user interface (UI) is not the main character. The main characters are the photos and videos of Vietnam. Our interface is merely a clear, refined layer of glass on top, allowing users to interact without interrupting their emotional connection to the scenery behind it.

* **Execution:** The **Glassmorphism** effect is the embodiment of this principle. It must create a sense of depth and subtle layering. Content blocks are not solid cards that obscure the beauty, but frosted panes of glass that allow the light and color of the landscape to shine through, creating a harmonious whole.

### **2\. The "Eloquence of Emptiness" Principle**

**Mindset:** Luxury doesn't come from filling every space, but from **knowing what to leave empty**. Every whitespace on our site is an intentional pause, a deep breath that allows the eye to rest and the mind to focus on what truly matters.

* **Execution:** Be generous with space. Let the typography and imagery "breathe." The layout must be airy, balanced, and rhythmic. Avoid clutter. Minimalism here does not mean empty; it means **distilled to the purest essence**.

### **3\. The "Typography as Voice" Principle**

**Mindset:** Fonts are not just for reading; they are for **feeling**. Our typography is the voice of the "Du Lich Viet" brand: confident, modern, and clear, but never cold. It guides the user's eye naturally through every story, every destination.

* **Execution:** The typographic hierarchy must be strong. Headings should be decisive and inviting. Paragraphs should be comfortable and smooth, like a quiet conversation. The contrast between font sizes and weights is the melody of our design.

### **4\. The "Gentle Motion" Principle**

**Mindset:** Every animation and interaction on the site should feel **natural and fluid**, like a gentle breeze or a soft ripple on the water. We don't use flashy, jarring effects to grab attention. Subtlety lies in motion that is almost invisible but makes the experience feel alive.

* **Execution:** On-scroll animations should be gentle. Hover states should provide immediate but soft feedback. The timing of all motion should be considered to create a sense of calm, not urgency.

## **Inspiration & Emotion**

* **Color Palette:** Think of the feeling of a clear morning in Ha Long Bay. It's the **blue of the sky and water**, the **white of the clouds and sand**, and a hint of **teal** where the sun hits the waves. Our palette must evoke that same sense of peace and freshness.  
* **Imagery:** This must be photographic art. Every picture must tell a story, possessing depth and emotion. Prioritize golden-hour moments, expansive landscapes, and authentic details.  
* **Icons:** Treat them like small signposts on a quiet trail. They appear only when absolutely necessary and must be so subtle that people notice their function without noticing their presence.

**In summary:** Design with emotion. Put yourself in the shoes of a traveler dreaming of their next journey. Every design decision must answer the question: "Does this make Vietnam even more captivating in their eyes?"
